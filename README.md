# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

An end-to-end HR Analytics dashboard built in Power BI, focused on employee attrition analysis.

---

## 🎯 Objective

Identify key factors contributing to employee attrition and help HR teams make data-driven decisions to improve retention.

---

## 📌 Key Metrics (KPIs)

- Total Employees
- Attrition Count & Attrition Rate (%)
- Average Age, Salary & Years at Company
- Active Employees

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- **Attrition by Education** — Field of study breakdown
- **Attrition by Age Group** — Which age bands have highest attrition
- **Attrition by Salary Slab** — Correlation between pay and leaving
- **Attrition by Years at Company** — Tenure vs attrition trend
- **Attrition by Job Role** — Role-wise attrition table
- **Gender-wise Attrition** — Side-by-side comparison
- **Department Filter (Slicer)** — HR / R&D / Sales

## 💡 Key Insights

- Employees in the **26–35 age group** show the highest attrition
- **Laboratory Technicians** and **Sales Executives** are the most affected roles
- Majority of attrition occurs in employees earning **up to ₹5K/month**
- Attrition peaks at **1 year** of tenure — onboarding & early engagement are critical
